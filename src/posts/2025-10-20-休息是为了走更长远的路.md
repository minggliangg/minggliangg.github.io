It’s been almost ten days since my last post. It might look like I’ve fallen off the wagon again — but that’s not the case. The past month has just been... eventful.

A while back, I joined a casual floorball game — something I hadn’t played in years and was really looking forward to. Unfortunately, midway through, I fractured my forefoot and ended up in a cast. The recovery period wasn’t exactly pleasant, especially when paired with some work-related burnout that had been creeping up on me. To be honest, things weren’t looking too great for a while.

But that downtime taught me something simple yet important: rest is part of progress. Healing — both physically and mentally — takes time, and that’s okay. Now that my cast is off, I’m slowly getting back on my feet, one small step at a time.

To help with recovery, I’ve been trying to build small, consistent habits. I recently explored a few wellness tools through a LinkedIn Premium promotion that included access to [Headspace](https://www.headspace.com/) and [Brilliant](https://brilliant.org/). They’ve been surprisingly helpful in easing back into focus and mindfulness. I’ve also been experimenting with using LLMs to get structured advice on how to manage burnout and find a better rhythm.

On the creative side, I decided to revisit something from my university days — a simple board game called _Breakthrough_ ([Wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakthrough_(board_game)>)). It was part of one of my first computing modules when I was pursuing my Physics degree. Back then, I implemented it in MATLAB but never quite finished the computer-player feature — a small, nagging loose end that stuck with me ever since. So this time, I’m rebuilding it from scratch (or rather, in React). It’s still a work in progress — the UI is rough and the game logic’s incomplete — but it feels good to be creating again.
You can check it out here: [react-breakthrough](https://github.com/minggliangg/react-breakthrough)

All in all, I think things are starting to look up.

休息不是终点，而是为了更好地前进。<br>
适当的休息，不只是为了走更长远的路，也是为了选择更好的路。
